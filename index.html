Commitment and terms

Payment

  • Pilot: 35% on signing (before production starts), 35% at the start of Month 2, 30% at the start of Month 3.
  • After the pilot: invoiced monthly in advance, due within 7 days.
  • Episode expenses are funded before each shoot and reconciled at actual cost monthly.
  • The programme fee for a month is earned once that month begins. GST applies on all invoices.

Responsibilities

Kaivalya Digitals: programme management and hosting, research and scripts, guest coordination, production supervision, post-production, publishing, distribution and monthly reporting; guest, audience and source permissions cleared before publication.

You: the working studio and equipment, one approval contact, channel ownership and account access, approved funds released on schedule and feedback within 3 working days.

Scope and ownership

  • Two review rounds per full episode and one per clip set. Extra rounds or scope changes are quoted in writing first.
  • The channel, accounts and published content belong to you once the related invoices are paid.
  • Paid interviews are always disclosed as commercial content. Business-idea segments do not promise investment or returns.
  • Valid for 30 days; final terms are confirmed in the signed agreement.
